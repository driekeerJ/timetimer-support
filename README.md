# Dial Timer — support

Dial Timer is a visual timer for Mac, iPhone and iPad: a coloured wedge on a dial
that shrinks as the minutes pass, several timers side by side, sequences, and
Focus blocks that ask two questions when they end.

## Getting help

Email **jpvdwal@gmail.com** with what you expected and what happened. A
screenshot helps.

## Common questions

**The alarm keeps ringing.** That is on purpose: it rings until you dismiss
it, so a timer that ends while you are away is not missed. Press Stop in the
alarm panel, or Escape on the Mac.

**I closed the window and the timers are gone.** They are not: on the Mac the
app keeps running in the menu bar and the Dock. Click the Dock icon, or the
menu bar item, to bring the window back.

**Can I start a timer from a script or a Shortcut?** On the Mac, yes:
open `timetimer://start?duration=25&name=Pasta`. Duration is in minutes.
`timetimer://pause`, `timetimer://reset` and `timetimer://stop-alarm` exist
too. A URL is acted on when it launches the app.

**Where is my data?** On your device, in the app's own preferences. Nothing is
sent anywhere. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
