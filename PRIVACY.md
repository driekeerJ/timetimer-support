# Privacy policy

_Last updated 21 September 2026_

Dial Timer does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no account and no sign-in.
- The app makes no network connections. It contains no analytics, no
  advertising and no crash-reporting service.
- Your timers, settings and focus log are stored only on your device, in the
  app's own preferences, and are deleted when you delete the app.
- The app asks for permission to show notifications so that a timer that ends
  while the app is in the background can still be heard. You can decline, and
  the app works without it.

If anything here changes, this page will say so, with the date above updated.
